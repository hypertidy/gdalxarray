BRAN2023 through kerchunk-Parquet references

kerchunk
Parquet
Zarr
THREDDS
ocean
A 13 TB ocean reanalysis on NCI THREDDS, read through a few megabytes of Parquet references.

BRAN2023, the Bluelink ocean reanalysis (CSIRO and the Bureau of Meteorology), is daily, 0.1 degree, 51 levels, from 1993: hundreds of terabytes of netCDF on NCI’s THREDDS server. kerchunk references in Parquet describe every chunk of every file, and GDAL’s Zarr driver reads them as one store. See Byte-reference stores for what such a reference table holds.

THREDDS limits aggressive parallel reads. Eight threads is a safe cap:

dask.config.set(scheduler="threads", num_workers=8)

Time comes through as numbers here: the references carry the values and units of the source, and decoding them is a one-liner if they are not decoded already. One day’s surface temperature over the Tasman Sea, chunked so dask reads the few chunks in parallel:

if live("bran-virtualized-pinned"):
    surf = (bran["temp"].isel(Time=0, st_ocean=0)
            .sel(yt_ocean=slice(-48, -25), xt_ocean=slice(145, 170))
            .chunk({"yt_ocean": 300, "xt_ocean": 300}).load())
    fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(7, 6))
    surf.plot(ax=ax, cmap="RdYlBu_r")
    ax.set_aspect(1 / np.cos(np.radians(-36)))
    plt.show()
